TradePulse Nepal — Processed Foreign Trade Data
Release: Shrawan FY 2083/84
Coverage: Mid July 2026 to Mid August 2026
Source: Department of Customs, Government of Nepal
Source page: https://customs.gov.np/category/summary-of-business-revenue-2083-084/?page=1

About these files
-----------------
These CSV files are processed from the official monthly foreign-trade workbook for Shrawan FY 2083/84.
TradePulse Nepal preserves the official source values and adds Rs-billion convenience columns where useful.
The Department of Customs workbook reports many monetary fields in Rs thousands.

Included datasets
-----------------
1. nepal-trade-summary-shrawan-fy2083-84.csv
   Headline imports, exports, trade deficit, total trade, import/export ratio, and trade shares.

2. nepal-import-products-shrawan-fy2083-84.csv
   4,014 HS-code import product rows with quantity, value, and import revenue.

3. nepal-export-products-shrawan-fy2083-84.csv
   490 HS-code export product rows with quantity and export value.

4. nepal-trade-by-country-shrawan-fy2083-84.csv
   107 partner-country rows with imports, exports, and trade balance.

5. nepal-trade-by-customs-office-shrawan-fy2083-84.csv
   28 customs-office rows with import/export values and shares.

6. nepal-import-product-partners-shrawan-fy2083-84.csv
   11,678 product-country import rows.

7. nepal-export-product-partners-shrawan-fy2083-84.csv
   1,483 product-country export rows.

8. nepal-trade-by-hs-chapter-shrawan-fy2083-84.csv
   96 HS chapter trade-balance rows.

Important notes
---------------
- Source total rows are excluded from the processed tables.
- HS codes are preserved as text so leading zeros remain intact.
- Rs-billion convenience columns equal the official Rs-thousand value divided by 1,000,000.
- TradePulse Nepal is an independent public-data project, not an official government publication.
- These files do not contain importer/exporter company names because those fields are not present in this monthly source workbook.
- For formal citation or legal/commercial decisions, verify figures against the original Department of Customs release.
