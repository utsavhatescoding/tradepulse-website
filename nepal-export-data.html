What the export CSV includes

HS code, product description, unit, quantity and export value. The partner file adds destination country for each product-country combination.

What it does not include

This monthly Customs workbook does not provide individual exporter or buyer company names. TradePulse does not invent or infer those fields.

About this Nepal export dataset

The Department of Customs source workbook reports export values in Rs thousands. TradePulse preserves that source value and adds an Rs-billion convenience column for analysis. Source total rows are removed from the downloadable product tables so each row represents an actual product or product-country observation.

HS codes are preserved as text so leading zeros remain intact when the CSV is opened in Excel, Google Sheets or analytical tools.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I download Nepal export data?

Use the CSV buttons on this page for Shrawan FY 2083/84 product-level and product-country export data. The downloads are free and require no account.

Does the export data include HS codes?

Yes. The product files retain the official 8-digit HS codes, descriptions, quantities and values from the source workbook.

Does this include Nepal exporter company names?

No. Company-level exporter and buyer names are not fields in this monthly source workbook.

Can I download all Nepal trade datasets together?

Yes. Use the complete Shrawan ZIP or visit the Nepal trade data download hub.
