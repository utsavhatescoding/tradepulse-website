How this report was produced

Cumulative figures come from the Department of Customs Bhadra workbook, covering Shrawan–Bhadra FY 2083/84. Bhadra-only movement is calculated by subtracting the Shrawan cumulative workbook.

TradePulse Nepal is an independent public-data project. Figures are rounded for presentation; the official workbook remains the authoritative record.
