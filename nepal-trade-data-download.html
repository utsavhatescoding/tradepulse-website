Processing notes

Preserve: source monetary values remain in Rs thousands.

Clean: source total rows are removed from detail tables.

Protect: HS codes stay as text so leading zeros remain intact.

Separate: official Bhadra data is cumulative; Bhadra-only movement is derived separately and never substituted into the source datasets.
